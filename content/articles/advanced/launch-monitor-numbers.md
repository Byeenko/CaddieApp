> [!key] Key points
> - **Ball data** (ball speed, launch, spin, carry) tells you *what* happened.
> - **Club data** (club speed, path, face, attack angle) tells you *why*.
> - **Smash factor** shows strike quality.
> - **Face-to-path** explains your curve.

## Ball data

| Number | What it is | Typical good ranges |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ball speed** | How fast the ball leaves the face | Driver: ~1.45–1.5× club speed |
| **Launch angle** | Vertical angle at take-off | Driver: ~12–16° for most amateurs; 7-iron: ~16–20° |
| **Spin rate** | Backspin in rpm | Driver: ~2,000–3,000; 7-iron: ~6,000–7,000 |
| **Spin axis** | Tilt of the spin; drives curve | Close to 0° = straight |
| **Carry** | Distance in the air | Your key number for approach shots |
| **Peak height / land angle** | How high, how steeply it lands | Steeper landing stops faster on greens |

## Club data

| Number | What it is | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Club speed** | Clubhead speed at impact | Typical male amateur driver speed ~90–95 mph; tour ~113–115 |
| **Smash factor** | Ball speed ÷ club speed | Driver max ≈ 1.50; irons lower. Low numbers = off-centre strikes |
| **Attack angle** | Up or down at impact | Driver: slightly positive (up) is ideal; irons: negative (down) |
| **Club path** | Direction of travel (in-to-out = +, out-to-in = −) | Combined with face, decides curve |
| **Face angle** | Where the face points at impact | Mostly decides start direction |
| **Face to path** | Face minus path | + = curves right (for a right-hander); − = curves left |
| **Dynamic loft** | Loft delivered at impact | Too much = high, weak shots; too little = low |

## Reading your numbers
- **Slice?** Look for a **negative path** with the face open to it. Fix the face first (see [[fixing-a-slice]]).
- **Low, spinning drives?** Check **attack angle** (probably negative) and **launch**. Try a higher tee and ball forward (see [[driver-setup]]).
- **Distance varies a lot with irons?** Check **smash factor** consistency. It's usually strike quality, not swing speed.

> [!mistake] Chasing one number
> Changing your swing to add 3 mph of club speed while your smash factor drops is a step backwards. Look at the whole picture: speed, strike and launch together.

> [!note] Estimated vs measured
> Some cheaper launch monitors and simulators measure the ball and **estimate** club data. Use those club numbers as a guide, not an exact truth.
