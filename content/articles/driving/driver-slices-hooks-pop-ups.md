> [!key] Key points
> - **Slice:** face open to the path, usually an out-to-in swing.
> - **Hook:** face closed to the path, usually an in-to-out swing or a strong grip.
> - **Pop-up / sky ball:** a steep, downward strike that hits the top of the face.
> - Check your **setup first**. Most driver faults start there.

## The slice
**What you see:** starts left or straight, curves hard right.
**Cause:** clubface open compared with the swing path, usually from an out-to-in, "over the top" path.

**Quick fixes for the driver:**
- Ball forward and spine tilted away (see [[driver-setup]]). If the ball is too far back or your shoulders are open, you swing across it.
- Strengthen your grip a touch.
- Feel the club approach from **inside**: tee a second ball a few centimetres outside and behind your ball, and miss it.

The full step-by-step is in [[fixing-a-slice]].

## The hook
**What you see:** starts right or straight, curves hard left.
**Cause:** clubface closed compared with the path, usually an in-to-out path combined with a face that shuts too quickly.

**Quick fixes:**
- **Grip check:** too strong (four knuckles showing)? Turn both hands slightly left.
- **Ball position:** too far back makes you swing out to the right, so move it forward.
- **Keep turning:** hooks often come when the body stops rotating and the hands flip the face over. Feel your chest keep turning towards the target through impact.

## The pop-up (sky ball)
**What you see:** very high, very short; a mark on the top of the club's crown.
**Cause:** the club comes down too steeply and the ball hits the top edge of the face.

**Quick fixes:**
- Check tee height. If you tee very high *and* swing down, you'll go under it.
- Keep your spine tilt and your head behind the ball. Lunging forward at the ball steepens the swing.
- Feel a wider, shallower swing. Sweep the ball off the tee rather than hitting down on it.

> [!tip] Diagnose by start and curve
> Watch where the ball **starts** (that's mostly the face) and which way it **curves** (that's face compared with path). It tells you what to fix, as explained in [[ball-flight-laws]].

> [!drill] Gate drill for path
> Place two tees just wider than your clubhead, a ball's width outside and inside the ball, angled the way you want the club to travel. Make half swings through the gate. Hitting the outer tee means your path is out to in (slice); hitting the inner tee means it's in to out (hook).
