> [!key] Key points
> - **Safety first:** never hit while anyone could be in range, and shout **"Fore!"** if your ball is heading towards someone.
> - **Leave the course as you found it:** divots, pitch marks, bunkers.
> - **Keep up** with the group in front, not just ahead of the group behind.
> - **Be still and quiet** while others play.

## Safety
- Never swing (even a practice swing) when someone is near you. Check behind you too.
- Wait until the group ahead is clearly **out of range**: off the green on par 3s, and well up the fairway on long holes.
- If your ball is heading anywhere near people, shout **"Fore!"** loudly and early. If you hear it, cover your head and turn away.

## Looking after the course

| Do | How |
| --- | --- |
| **Replace divots**, or fill them | Put the turf back and press it down, or fill the hole with the sand/seed mix if your course provides it |
| **Repair pitch marks** on the green | Push the edges in towards the centre with a repair tool, then tap the surface flat. Don't lever the middle up |
| **Rake bunkers** | Enter and leave from the low side, rake your footprints and the shot mark, and leave the rake where your course asks |
| **Keep carts** off greens, tees and wet areas | Follow the signs. "90-degree rule" days mean stay on the path and cut straight across to your ball |

## On the green
- Don't walk on another player's **putting line** (the path between their ball and the hole), or just past the hole.
- Don't stand **directly behind** someone putting, or behind the hole in their line of sight.
- Leave your bag or cart on the side of the green **nearest the next tee**.
- Tend or remove the flag for others if asked, and replace it carefully.
- **Write scores at the next tee**, not on the green.

## Pace of play
A round of 18 should take about **4 hours** for a group of four (less for twos and threes).

- **Play ready golf.** Be ready to hit when it's your turn: work out your yardage and choose your club while others play.
- **Keep your place**, which is right behind the group ahead. If a gap opens up, speed up.
- **Let faster groups through** if you're holding them up, especially if you're searching for a ball.
- **Limit practice swings** to one.
- **Hit a provisional** when in doubt, so you don't walk back (see [[essential-rules]]).
- **Pick up** in Stableford once you can't score on a hole.

> [!tip] Be a good playing partner
> Watch other players' shots so you can help find balls. Stay quiet and still in their line of sight. Keep phones on silent. A quick "good shot" goes a long way.

## Dress and the clubhouse
Dress codes vary by club. Collared shirts, tailored shorts or trousers and golf shoes are typical. Check the club's website before your first visit. Most clubs ask you to remove your hat and spikes inside the clubhouse.
