> [!key] Key points
> - Track a **few simple stats**: fairways, greens, putts, up-and-downs, penalties.
> - Note the **three costliest shots** of the round, and why.
> - Turn the review into **one practice priority** for the week.
> - Look for **patterns over several rounds**, not single results.

## Simple stats to keep

| Stat | How to count it | What it tells you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fairways hit** | Par 4s and 5s where the tee shot finished in the fairway | Driving accuracy |
| **Greens in regulation (GIR)** | On the green in par minus 2 shots (or better) | Approach play |
| **Putts** | Total putts; also count 3-putts | Putting, especially speed |
| **Up-and-downs** | Missed green, then in the hole in 2 | Short game |
| **Penalty strokes** | Every penalty: OB, water, unplayable | Strategy and driving |

Mark them on your scorecard with small ticks as you play, or use an app.

## The three costliest shots
After the round, identify the three shots that cost you the most. For each one:
1. **What happened?** (e.g. "drove OB on the 7th")
2. **Why?** Technique, decision, or bad luck? ("Hit driver when 3-wood was the smart play.")
3. **What would fix it?** ("Leave driver in the bag on the 7th.")

Often the answer is a **decision**, not a swing fault, and decisions are free to fix.

## From review to practice
Look across your last five rounds:
- **Lots of penalty shots?** Work on strategy and a "fairway finder" (see [[finding-fairways]]).
- **Many 3-putts?** Lag putting (see [[lag-putting]]).
- **Low up-and-down %?** Short-game drills (see [[short-game-drills]]).
- **Few greens from short range?** Wedge distances (see [[pitching-basics]]).

Then set the next week's focus in your [[building-a-practice-plan|practice plan]].

> [!tip] Go deeper with Strokes Gained
> For a more precise picture of where you gain and lose shots against golfers of your level, see [[strokes-gained]]. Shot-tracking apps can calculate it for you.

> [!mistake] Remembering only the bad
> Write down three things that went well first. It keeps the review honest, and it reminds you what to keep doing.
