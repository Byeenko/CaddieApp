> [!key] Key points
> - Start the club back **together**: hands, arms and shoulders as one unit.
> - At hip height the shaft should be **parallel to the ground and to your toe line**.
> - The **toe of the club points roughly up** at that point, with the clubface matching your spine angle.
> - Keep it **smooth**. A snatched takeaway ruins your tempo.

## Why the takeaway matters

The takeaway only lasts a fraction of a second, but it sets up the path and clubface for the rest of the swing. Get it right and the backswing almost builds itself. Get it wrong and you'll be making compensations all the way down.

## The one-piece takeaway

Think of the triangle formed by your arms and shoulders at address. In the takeaway, that **triangle moves back together**, driven by your chest and shoulders turning, not by your hands.

1. Start by **turning your chest** away from the target.
2. The club, hands and arms move with it. There's **very little wrist hinge** in the first couple of feet.
3. The clubhead stays **outside your hands** for the first part of the takeaway.

## The checkpoint: hip height

Stop when your hands reach about hip height and check:

| Check | What you want |
| --- | --- |
| Shaft | Parallel to the ground, and parallel to your toe line |
| Clubface | Toe pointing up, or the leading edge angled to match your spine |
| Hands | In front of your chest, not behind your body |
| Clubhead | Slightly outside your hands, not tucked behind you |

> [!mistake] Rolling the face open
> Rolling your forearms early whips the club behind you with the face pointing at the sky. You then have to re-route the club in the downswing, which usually means coming over the top. Keep the face "looking at the ball" a little longer.

> [!mistake] Picking it up
> Lifting the club with your hands and arms, with no body turn, makes the swing narrow and steep. Let the turn move the club.

> [!drill] Mirror at hip height
> Face a mirror (or your phone camera, set up face-on). Make slow takeaways to hip height and stop. Check the shaft is parallel to the ground and the toe points up. Then turn side-on and check the shaft is parallel to your toes. Ten slow reps, then hit balls making normal swings.

> [!drill] Club across the chest
> Hold a club across your chest with both arms crossed. Turn back so the shaft points out in front of you. Your chest should turn without your hips sliding sideways. That's the turn that powers the takeaway.
