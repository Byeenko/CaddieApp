> [!key] Key points
> - **Eyes over the ball**, or just inside it, so you see the line properly.
> - **Ball slightly forward** of centre, under your lead eye.
> - **Arms hang** from the shoulders; bend from the hips.
> - **Aim the putter face first**, then set your body parallel to the line.

## Why setup matters so much in putting
The putting stroke is small and slow, so there's no time to correct anything during it. Wherever your setup points the face and the stroke, that's where the ball goes. Small setup errors are the biggest cause of missed short putts.

## Building your setup
1. **Posture:** bend from the hips with a little knee flex, as with a full swing but more upright. Let your arms hang so your hands sit under your shoulders.
2. **Eyes:** directly over the ball or slightly inside it (on your side of the line). Check by holding a ball against the bridge of your nose and dropping it. It should land on, or just inside, your ball.
3. **Ball position:** slightly forward of centre, under your lead eye. This lets you catch the ball with the putter just past the bottom of the stroke, so it rolls rather than skids.
4. **Stance:** about shoulder-width or a little narrower, feet parallel to the line.
5. **Weight:** balanced, or slightly favouring the lead side. You shouldn't feel any movement in your lower body.
6. **Putter:** sole flat on the ground. If the toe or heel sticks up, your posture (or the putter's length) is off.

## Grip options

| Grip | How | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Reverse overlap** (conventional) | Lead index finger over the trail fingers | Most golfers; good feel |
| **Cross-handed** (lead hand low) | Lead hand below the trail hand | Levelling the shoulders; quieting a flicky trail hand |
| **Claw** | Trail hand rests on the grip like holding a pencil | Players whose trail hand gets jumpy under pressure |
| **Arm-lock** | Longer putter rests against the lead forearm | Keeping the wrists completely out of it |

Whatever the grip, hold the putter **lightly**, with the palms facing each other, and keep the pressure constant through the stroke.

## Aim
1. From behind the ball, pick your start line (see [[reading-greens]]) and a spot just in front of the ball on that line.
2. Set the putter face square to that spot.
3. Then set your feet and shoulders parallel to the line.

> [!mistake] Shoulders open
> Many golfers set up with their shoulders aiming left of the line, and the stroke follows the shoulders. Lay a club across your shoulders to check. It should be parallel to your putting line.

> [!drill] Chalk line or mirror
> Use a putting mirror or a chalk line on the practice green. Check that your eyes sit over the line and your shoulders are parallel to it before every practice putt. A week of this sets the habit.

> [!tip] Putter length
> Many off-the-shelf putters are too long for the posture above, which pushes your eyes inside the line and the hands too high. If your arms can't hang naturally, a putter fitting (or a shorter putter) can help.
