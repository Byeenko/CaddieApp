> [!key] Key points
> - Practise **what costs you the most shots**. Find out from your round reviews.
> - Spend **at least half** your practice time on the **short game and putting**.
> - Every session needs **one clear goal**.
> - Mix **technique** practice with **game-like** practice under pressure.

## Find your priorities
Before planning practice, look at your last few rounds (see [[reviewing-your-round]]):
- Penalty shots from tee shots? → driving accuracy
- Lots of three-putts? → lag putting
- Missing greens from short range? → wedges
- Duffed chips? → chipping technique

Shots from inside 100 yards, including putts, make up **around 60%** of all shots for most club golfers. That's where the quickest gains usually are.

## A sample weekly plan
*Example for someone with about 2–3 hours a week. Adjust it to your own priorities.*

| Session | Time | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Short game** | 45 min | 15 min chipping, 15 min pitching, 15 min bunker or up-and-down game |
| **2. Putting** (can be at home) | 30 min | 10 min start line, 10 min lag putting, 10 min pressure drill |
| **3. Full swing** | 45–60 min | 15 min technique on one thing, 30 min target and routine practice |
| **Round** | 9 or 18 holes | Process goals: routine, targets, smart choices |

## Structure every session
1. **Warm up** (5 minutes): stretches and short shots.
2. **Technique block** (one thing): slow, deliberate reps with feedback, e.g. alignment sticks, a mirror, impact spray.
3. **Transfer block:** hit shots like on the course, changing club and target every ball, with your full routine.
4. **Test:** finish with a scored drill so you can track progress.

> [!mistake] The bucket-of-drivers session
> Hitting 100 drivers as fast as possible feels like practice, but it mostly grooves a rushed tempo. Fewer balls, each with a purpose, beats volume.

> [!tip] Keep a practice log
> Write down the date, the focus, and your test score each session (e.g. "1.5-metre circle drill: 14/18"). Seeing numbers improve keeps you motivated and shows what's working.

> [!drill] Combine (test) day
> Once a month, run the same test: 10 putts from 1, 2 and 3 metres; 10 chips to a hole; 10 wedges to a 15-yard target; 10 drives at a 30-yard "fairway". Score each section and compare month to month.
