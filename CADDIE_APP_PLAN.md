# Caddie App: Project Plan

*Status: idea stage, not started yet. Captured 21 Nov 2026.*

## The vision

An interactive app that teaches you **everything** about golf, all in one place: the swing, stance, lies, mentality and more. It also helps you live while you're playing a round.

The problem it solves: golf knowledge is scattered across YouTube, coaches and forums. This app pulls it all together and makes it interactive instead of just a textbook.

---

## Two main sections

### 1. Off the Course (the Library)

Everything you learn, study and practise away from a round.

**Content modules**

- **Swing**: grip, setup, takeaway, transition, impact, follow-through
- **Stance & Setup**: ball position, alignment, posture for each club
- **Lies**: uphill, downhill, sidehill (ball above/below feet), rough, bunkers, plugged, hardpan, divots
- **Short Game**: chipping, pitching, bunker play, putting and green reading
- **Course Management**: club selection, when to attack vs play safe, playing the wind, missing on the right side
- **Mentality / Mental Game**: pre-shot routine, bouncing back from a bad hole, first-tee nerves, staying focused over 18
- **Rules & Etiquette**: the stuff that trips up newer players
- **Equipment**: what actually matters at each level
- **Real-World Examples**: what tour pros do, plus proven drills and fixes that work for everyday golfers (e.g. curing a slice, stopping chunked chips)

**Features**

- Interactive lessons, e.g. drag a slider to change ball position and see how ball flight changes, or tap a lie type to see how to set up for it
- Drills and practice plans
- **Player profile**: your typical distance per club, your common misses, what you're currently working on

### 2. On the Course (the Live Caddie)

Real-time help during a round.

- **Quick input:** tell it your situation in a few taps or by voice
  - e.g. *"150 out, ball above my feet, light breeze into me, 7 or 8 iron?"*
- **It responds with:**
  - Club recommendation
  - Setup adjustments for the lie
  - Where to aim
  - A simple swing thought
- **Mental support between shots:** resetting after a double bogey, sticking to your routine when you're playing well
- **Personalised:** uses your own club distances and tendencies from the Off the Course profile

## Important considerations

### Rules of Golf: Competition Mode
Under the Rules of Golf, getting advice from a device during a **competition** round isn't allowed, and club recommendations count as advice. Casual rounds and practice are fine.

**Plan:** add a **"Comp Mode"** toggle that switches off the live caddie (or limits it to distances only) so users don't get in trouble at club events.

### Real-world examples and licensing
Using pro tour footage directly would run into licensing problems. **Plan:** describe pro techniques in our own words and diagrams, and link out to official sources where relevant.

## Open questions (to decide before building)

- **Who is it for?** Complete beginners, mid-handicappers trying to break 90, or both, with different learning paths?
- **Platform:** iPhone app, web app, or both?
- **How interactive?** Which lessons get sliders/visuals first?
- **Live caddie input:** taps, voice, or both? Does it use GPS for distances?
- **Business model:** free, paid, or subscription?
