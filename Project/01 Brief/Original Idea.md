# Original Idea

*Captured from the first chat with Claude, 20–21 Nov 2026. Kept for history; [[Product Requirements v1]] is the current brief.*

## The spark (20 Nov, late evening)

> An interactive app that teaches you **everything** golf: aspects of the swing, stance, lies. Literally everything, all in one place.

## How it grew in the same conversation

1. **Scope:** swing, stance and setup, lies, short game, putting, course management, **mental game**, rules and etiquette, equipment, and **real-world examples of what works** (tour players, proven drills).
2. **Real-time help:** while playing a round, tell the app your situation and it helps you out.
3. **Two sections:** **Off the course** (the library) and **On the course** (the live caddie).

## Points raised at the time

- **Rules of Golf:** advice from a device in a *competition* round isn't allowed, and club recommendations count as advice. Plan a comp mode. Became [[DEC-008 GPS caddie approach and comp mode]].
- **Tour footage:** can't use pro video directly because of licensing. Describe techniques in our own words and diagrams instead.
