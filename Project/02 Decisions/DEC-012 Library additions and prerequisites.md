# DEC-012: Library additions and prerequisites

## Decision
- **New category: Golf Fitness** (mobility, strength and speed, injury prevention). Fitness was the main gap in the original structure. Lessons say clearly they're general information and point to a physio for pain or injury.
- **New topic: Weight Transfer** in Swing Mechanics, as asked for in the Phase 3 brief.
- **Prerequisites:** lessons can list `prereq` lessons, shown as "Start with" links at the top.
- **Every topic and subtopic has at least one lesson** (83 lessons in 16 categories at v0.3.0). The build script now warns about broken links, missing images and unknown visual types.
