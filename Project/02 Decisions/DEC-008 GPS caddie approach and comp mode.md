# DEC-008: GPS caddie approach and comp mode

## Direction agreed
- **Version 1 is distance-only**: front, centre and back of the green, plus carry and lay-up distances to bunkers and water, in yards.
- **Course data:** download green, bunker and water outlines for *your* courses from OpenStreetMap and save them on the phone, so it works offline. Fill any gaps by marking points ourselves.
- No live map tiles needed. The hole can be drawn from the saved outlines.
- **Comp mode:** club recommendations and advice count as "advice" under the Rules of Golf and aren't allowed in competition. Distance-only is allowed unless a local rule bans devices. Advice features stay behind a comp-mode switch.
